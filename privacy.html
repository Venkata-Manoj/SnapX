Summary

Your camera frames, microphone audio and room pictures are processed on your computer and never uploaded. If you create an account, we store only what is needed to sign you in, run your plan and take payment.

{{BRAND_NAME}} is a Chrome extension. The extension works without an account on the Free plan, and that use sends nothing to us.

The extension

On-device processing

Camera frames and mic audio are processed in memory on your device for snap detection, person cutout, room rebuild and the Dust effect. Nothing is uploaded. The AI models are bundled inside the extension, so no server is involved.

What it stores on your device

Your settings are saved with chrome.storage, along with a learned picture of your room for each site and camera, so it can start faster next time. You can clear the room at any time in the popup: Room → Forget room. Removing the extension deletes everything.

What it sends when you sign in

If you sign in, the extension may contact {{SUPABASE_HOST}} and {{SITE_HOST}} to check your plan and fetch a signed plan pass. It sends your account token for that check. It never sends video, audio or images. Signed out, the extension makes no network requests at all.

Your account

An account is optional. You need one to buy or manage a paid plan, or to connect the extension to your plan.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • Your name and profile picture, if your sign-in provider shares them.
  • How you signed in — Google, Microsoft, Apple or an email code.
  • Your plan, subscription status, and the dates it started, renews or ends.
  • Payment IDs and dates for charges and refunds.

What we do not collect

  • Card, UPI or bank details. Our payment partner handles those, and we only receive the result: your plan, its status and payment IDs.
  • Your video, audio, room pictures, call content or contact lists.

The website

You can read every public page without an account. Our host keeps standard request logs — IP address, browser and time — for security and debugging, under its own retention policy. Sign-in on this site is handled by Supabase Auth.
