Summary

SnapX processes your camera and microphone only on your computer, inside Chrome, to detect snaps and hide you on video calls. It does not collect, store on any server, sell or share personal data.

What it processes, and where

Camera frames and mic audio are processed in memory on your device for snap detection, person cutout, room rebuild and the dust effect. Nothing is uploaded.

What it stores on your device

Your settings are saved with chrome.storage, along with a learned picture of your room for each site and camera, so it can start faster next time. You can clear the room at any time in the popup: Room → Forget room. Removing the extension deletes everything.

Network

The extension makes no network requests. AI models are bundled inside the extension.

Permissions explained

storage saves your settings. offscreen runs the AI models in a background page. Site access is limited to Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom and X call pages, where SnapX provides its processed camera.

Diagnostics

"Copy diagnostics" copies technical details — versions, settings, frame rate and error messages, never images or audio — to your clipboard. It's only shared if you choose to send it.
