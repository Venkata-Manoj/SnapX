1.5.1

Current release

The welcome now lives in the popup with a NEW badge. Floating controls shrink to a dot, and a Show floating controls button brings them back. A clearer plan status, with Refresh plan. Signing out on the website now removes the plan from that computer.

1.5.0

Five effects, live now. Dust is free on Google Meet and Zoom. Ember, Glitch, Portal and Fade are included in Pro and Max, along with Teams and X calls and the Be right back freeze-frame. Max also gets early access to labs features. A three-day renewal grace keeps Pro and Max active while a renewal charge and its webhook catch up.

1.4.0

New look. A redesigned popup in Paper & Vermilion with Effect, Controls, Room and About tabs, a new logo and icons, a hidden-time timer, and a clearer HD status when your computer has no GPU.

1.3.3

Fixed a faint coloured ghost on bright, overexposed walls. The rebuilt room now matches the camera even when the wall is blown out to white.

1.3.2

Smooth video on laptops without GPU acceleration, because the AI runs in the background instead of holding frames back. HD runs only on a GPU (WebGPU), and the popup says why when it can't. Auto no longer loads the HD model on machines that can't use it. Fixed grain measurement on denoised webcams.

1.3.1

No more see-through ghost while hidden: holes inside your outline are closed and the cover holds longer. Ghost lock: the learned room can't absorb your shirt or shoulders. The rebuilt area wears your camera's own grain. Cutout changes apply instantly. Added Copy diagnostics.

1.3.0

Learn room in 3 seconds. Exposure tracking. HD person cutout (MODNet) on capable GPUs. GPU room rebuild. Smarter "camera moved" detection. Security hardening.

1.2.2

X video calls are supported. The room learned with nobody in view is kept for good. {{BRAND_NAME}} relearns the room if the camera moves.

1.2.1

Remembers your room per site and camera. Room learning ignores webcam brightness changes. Removed "Hold camera brightness".

1.2

Live colour matching, soft edge blending, shadow catch, edge snapping, halo clean-up.

1.1

Ghost fix while learning, fists and raised hands stay hidden, better snap detection.

1.0

First release. Snap to dust on Meet, Teams and Zoom.
