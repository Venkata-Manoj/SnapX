HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. One snap.

  1. 01

    Learn your room

    Click Learn room and step out of view for 3 seconds. {{BRAND_NAME}} remembers what's behind you, and on-device AI fills in any spot you always cover.

  2. 02

    Snap

    Snap your fingers where the camera can see your hand. You break into thousands of particles and drift away. Your mic mutes.

  3. 03

    Snap back

    Snap again and the dust flies back into you. Your mic unmutes, unless you were already muted.

No hand free? Press Alt Shift S or click Vanish now in the popup.

UNDER THE HOOD

Built like a camera pipeline, not a filter.

SNAP DETECTION

The camera sees thumb and middle finger snap together, and the mic hears the click at the same moment.

PERSON CUTOUT

On-device segmentation (MediaPipe). HD uses MODNet on your GPU for cleaner hair and edges.

ROOM LEARNING

Every pixel you don't cover builds a picture of the room. AI inpainting (MI-GAN) fills the rest.

COLOUR MATCH

The rebuilt room is re-coloured every frame to your webcam's exposure and white balance, so no ghost is left.

DUST

A WebGL effect splits you into about 26,000 particles.

AUDIO

While you're hidden, the audio sent to the call is silent, even if the meeting's own button can't be found.

Add to Chrome

System requirements

  • Chrome 116 or newer, on Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS
  • A webcam, and a microphone for the snap-sound check (optional)
  • Fast cutout works on any computer
  • HD cutout needs a GPU with graphics acceleration on (chrome://settings/system → "Use graphics acceleration when available")

First-use tips

  • Learn your room once: click Learn room, then step fully out of view for 3 seconds.
  • Plain walls and steady light give the cleanest result.
  • Keep the camera still. If you move it, {{BRAND_NAME}} relearns the room.
  • Snap a little away from your face. Side-on snaps work too.

HELP

If something's off.

Popup says "Not on a call tab"

Open Meet, Teams, Zoom or X in Chrome and reload the tab.

"Waiting for your camera"

Turn your camera off and on in the meeting.

Snaps are missed

Keep your hand in view, a little away from your face. Raise sensitivity, or turn off the click-sound check if your mic is quiet.

You vanish by accident

Lower the sensitivity and keep the click-sound check on.

A faint outline where you sat

Open the Room tab, click Learn room, and step out for 3 seconds. Plain walls work best.

HD is crossed out

Your computer has no GPU path (WebGPU). Fast is used automatically. Try turning on graphics acceleration in chrome://settings/system.

Video is slow

Turn on graphics acceleration in chrome://settings/system and relaunch Chrome.

Mic button didn't turn red

The meeting may have changed its layout. {{BRAND_NAME}} still silences your audio itself.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

The Free plan is free forever: Dust on Google Meet and Zoom. Pro and Max unlock all five effects, Teams and X, and Be right back.

Which effects are included?

All five effects are live: Dust, Ember, Glitch, Portal and Fade. Dust is free on Meet and Zoom. The other four need Pro or Max.

What's planned for 1.6?

Planned, not promised: sound packs for each effect, wave and double-snap gestures, teleport, per-site settings, rounded cutouts and themes.

Does it record or upload anything?

No video, audio or images are ever uploaded. If you sign in, {{BRAND_NAME}} sends only your account token to check your plan.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account page, and you keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for.

Which payment methods?

UPI, cards and netbanking in India.

Will other people see a glitch or an outline?

In good light with a learned room, they see your empty room. Busy backgrounds can look softer where you were. Learning the room once makes the biggest difference.

Do I need a GPU?

No. Fast mode works on any computer. HD mode, with cleaner hair and edges, needs a GPU.

Does it work in the Zoom, Teams or Meet desktop apps?

Not yet. {{BRAND_NAME}} works in Chrome on the web versions.

Does my mic really mute?

Yes. While you're hidden, the audio {{BRAND_NAME}} passes to the call is silent. It can also press the meeting's own mute button so others see you as muted.

What if I snap by accident?

Lower the sensitivity in the Controls tab and keep the click-sound check on. Snap again to come back.

Can I use a shortcut instead of snapping?

Yes. Press Alt+Shift+S, or change the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Is it on the Chrome Web Store?

Yes. Install it from the Chrome Web Store.

Not yet. {{BRAND_NAME}} is coming to the Chrome Web Store; install it from there when it arrives.

Is it okay to use in meetings?

It's made for fun and quick breaks. Use it kindly, and don't use it to fake attendance.
