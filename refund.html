First payment refund

If you are not happy with a paid plan, ask for a refund of your first payment within {{REFUND_WINDOW_DAYS}} days of the charge. We refund it in full. This is a goodwill policy on top of any rights you have under Indian consumer law.

Renewals

Renewal payments are not refundable, except when:

  • you were charged twice for the same period (a duplicate charge); or
  • a technical failure on our side stopped the paid features from working, we could not fix it, and you tell us before the next renewal.

Cancelling stops future charges. It does not refund a renewal that has already been taken, unless one of the exceptions above applies.
